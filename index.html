Please listen with headphones. All examples are binaural; loudspeaker playback may remove the spatial cues.

Conditions

Each example renders the same dry source in the same room and listener pose with four methods.

  • 1Reference Full-order geometric simulator

    Full-order geometric simulation without ray pruning; the target that RayBRIR approximates.

  • 2RayBRIR proposed

    Learned ray pruning (MLP1) followed by directional energy compensation (MLP2).

  • 3RayBRIR w/o MLP2ablation

    Same ray pruning (MLP1), but the energy removed by pruning is not compensated.

  • 4Anchor HRTF only

    Source convolved with the HRTF only: no reflections, no reverberation.

What to listen for

  1. 1vs2
    RayBRIR is very close to the full-order reference.

    Reverberance, spaciousness and source position should be hard to tell apart.

  2. 2vs3
    Removing MLP2 changes the perceived reverberation time.

    Without energy compensation, the reverberant tail decays faster and the room sounds drier. It is easiest to hear in speech pauses and at note endings.

  3. 4vs123
    The anchor has no room at all.

    It sounds dry and close to the head. The contrast shows the environmental cues (reverberation, distance, externalization) that the simulated BRIRs add.
